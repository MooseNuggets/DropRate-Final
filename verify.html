Verify a pull

Don't trust us — re-run it yourself. Enter a pull ID and your browser will fetch the drand randomness straight from a public relay, recompute the rarity from the published odds, and prove the result was fixed before the randomness ever existed. Nothing here trusts DropRate's servers for the math.

Tip: your pull IDs are on your My Wins receipts, or share a direct link like verify.html?id=42.
—

How it works: the pull's rarity is u = sha256("seed:nonce") mapped through the crate's published odds, where seed is the randomness from a specific future drand round committed the moment you paid — before that randomness existed. This page fetches that round from api.drand.sh itself, checks it equals the seed on record, and rolls the dice again locally. Same inputs, same result, every time.
