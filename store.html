How ownership works

Three steps
01

Buy it, own it

The copy is yours the moment you buy it — nobody can take it back or switch it off. Sign in on the web or in the desktop app and your library is right there.

02

Resell anytime

Finished with a game? List it back on the market. Open editions trade freely; limited runs build a real second-hand economy with a floor that protects value. The developer decides which.

03

Devs keep earning

Every resale sends a cut back to the studio — something second-hand discs never did. Buying pre-owned here still pays the people who built the game.
