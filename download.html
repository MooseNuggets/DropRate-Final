Three steps, then you're playing

Setup
01
⬇

Install

Download the installer and run it. It's a normal Windows app — no admin rights, no background services, nothing running when it's closed.

02
🔗

Pair with your wallet

The app shows a short code. Open droprate.xyz/pair, connect the wallet you buy games with, type the code — done. Your wallet's private key never touches the app.

03
▶

Play

Everything that wallet owns appears in your library. Click a game, it installs, click again, it runs. Buy something new from the store tab and it's there instantly.

Shipping a game?

Everything a studio needs lives in the Developer Portal: list your game, upload builds, set the price and supply, choose USDC or SOL, watch sales, withdraw earnings. The app has a Developer entry that opens it — same portal, same login, so there's exactly one place to manage a launch.
