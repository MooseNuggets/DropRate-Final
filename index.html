04 // Player FAQ

Questions

Do I need to register, stake, or click anything?

No. That's the point. Hold 10,000+ $DROP in a normal wallet and you're automatically in every draw. The only people who fill out a form are non-holders using the free entry lane.

When exactly are snapshots taken?

Wouldn't you like to know. (So would snipers.) Snapshots fire at random, unannounced times, and you need a clean consecutive streak to qualify — so the only winning strategy is the boring one: actually holding.

What happens if I sell?

Cooldown. Selling any amount — or moving tokens out — pauses your eligibility until a clean, unbroken holding streak covers the cooldown window. Buying more never hurts you. Diamond hands are the meta.

What game will I win?

Nobody knows. Keys are encrypted before anyone on the team can read them and only unsealed by the winner's wallet signature. Might be legendary. Might be a very sincere farming game. That reveal moment is yours alone.

How do I know a draw wasn't rigged?

Each draw commits to a future round of public randomness — run by Cloudflare, EPFL and the League of Entropy — before that randomness exists. Snapshot, seed, and winners are all published, and the selection script is open source. Run it yourself; you'll get the same winners or we've got a very public problem.

What if a winner never claims?

After 7 days the key returns to the pool and a replacement winner is drawn — same seed, deterministic, still verifiable. Mystery keys wait for no one.
